# Skyvan Business Domain

Status: Active

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document explains WHAT Skyvan is.

Not how the code works.

Not how the database works.

It explains the business itself.

Every engineering decision should align with this business model.

---

# Vision

Skyvan is building the operating system of the caravan industry.

The platform combines:

• Marketplace

• AI

• Administration

• CRM

• Content Management

• Revision Tracking

• Workflow Automation

The marketplace is only one module.

The long-term vision extends far beyond listings.

---

# Primary Users

## Visitors

Can:

- browse listings
- search
- compare
- contact sellers

Cannot:

- modify platform data

---

## Dealers

Can:

- create vehicles
- manage inventory
- edit offers
- manage media

---

## Administrators

Responsible for:

- platform management
- content moderation
- revisions
- approvals
- AI supervision

Admin is considered a production environment.

Safety has priority over speed.

# Business Philosophy

Every piece of data should have:

ownership

history

traceability

recoverability

Nothing important should disappear permanently.

---

# Revision Philosophy

Skyvan values historical accuracy.

Changes should be recoverable.

Future architecture should support:

version history

approval workflows

audit trails

---

# AI Philosophy

AI assists users.

AI never replaces business rules.

AI suggestions require validation.

AI should reduce repetitive work.

AI should never silently modify production data.

---

# Marketplace Philosophy

Quality over quantity.

Consistent data.

Reliable information.

Searchable structure.

High quality media.

SEO-friendly content.

---

# Administration Philosophy

Admin tools exist to maintain quality.

Every admin action should be understandable.

Critical operations should be traceable.

Large destructive operations should be reversible.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Engineers build infrastructure.

The infrastructure serves the business.

Business requirements always drive architecture.

Never build technology without business value.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Skyvan should become the central platform connecting:

Customers

Dealers

Manufacturers

Service Providers

Administrators

AI

through one consistent system.
